# AIX Center at SUNY Polytechnic Institute

Public website for the Artificial Intelligence Exploration (AIX) Center.

---

## About

The AIX Center develops AI literacy curriculum, applied research in human-AI interaction, and institutional AI adoption frameworks. We partner with universities across West Africa and work closely with SUNY system leadership on AI policy and pedagogy.

Current projects include AI literacy workshops, the Herkimer County AI Training and Policy Initiative, the T-DAC geopolitical analysis framework, and university partnerships in Nigeria and Cameroon.
