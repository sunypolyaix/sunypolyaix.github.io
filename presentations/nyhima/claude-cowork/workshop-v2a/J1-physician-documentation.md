## situation

A hospitalist physician consistently submits incomplete discharge summaries—missing final diagnoses, no follow-up plan, inadequate clinical reasoning. You've sent reminders. The medical staff office has sent reminders. His response: "I document what matters clinically. The rest is administrative burden that doesn't help my patients." He has the highest patient satisfaction scores in the department.

## source

Joint Commission Standard RC.02.01.01:
"The medical record contains information that reflects the patient's care, treatment, and services."

Required discharge summary elements include: reason for hospitalization, significant findings, procedures performed, condition at discharge, discharge instructions, follow-up plan, and attending physician signature.

Your hospital's medical staff bylaws state that incomplete records after 30 days constitute a delinquency and may result in suspension of admitting privileges.

The physician in question has 47 delinquent records. He has been practicing for 22 years and is widely regarded as an exceptional clinician.
