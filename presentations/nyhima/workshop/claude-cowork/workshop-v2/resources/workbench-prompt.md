# The Workbench Prompt

This is the most important prompt in the workshop. You run it at the end of
every round. It turns a messy back-and-forth with a model into a clean record
you can save, share, and reuse.

---

## The prompt — copy this exactly

> Generate a markdown summary of what we did here, starting with my first
> prompt. Identify the questions I asked and how we arrived at the final
> product. Include the final artifact verbatim. I want to copy this into a
> Google Doc to share with colleagues.

---

## Why this matters

A chat with a model is a conversation, not a document. It scrolls away. It is
hard to share. Half of it is you figuring out what you wanted.

This prompt asks the model to do three things at once:

1. **Retrace the path** — what you asked first, what you adjusted, how you got
   to the end. This is the record of your thinking, not just the model's.
2. **Keep the final product whole** — "verbatim" means the model copies the
   finished memo, guidance doc, or summary exactly, with nothing dropped or
   reworded.
3. **Hand you something portable** — markdown pastes cleanly into Google Docs,
   Word, or email. You leave with a file, not a browser tab.

---

## How to use it

1. Do your work in the round — read, think, write — until you have something
   you would actually use.
2. Paste the workbench prompt as your next message.
3. Read what comes back. Make sure your final artifact is there in full.
4. Copy it into a Google Doc, Word file, or email. That is your artifact.

---

## If the model leaves something out

Sometimes the model summarizes the final artifact instead of copying it whole.
If that happens, send one more message:

> You summarized the final artifact. Paste it back in full, exactly as you
> wrote it, with nothing shortened.

---

## What you walk away with

After every round you have a single document that contains your prompts, the
model's reasoning, and the finished product. Stack two of these together and
you have the record of a full working session — reproducible, shareable, and
yours.
