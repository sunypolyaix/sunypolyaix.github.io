## The document — paste this into your first model

> **42 CFR § 482.24 — Condition of Participation: Medical Record Services**
> *(paraphrased for training)*
>
> The hospital must have a medical record service that maintains a record for
> every patient.
>
> - **Organization.** The service must have administrative responsibility for
>   medical records, and records must be promptly completed, filed, retained,
>   and accessible.
> - **Form and retention.** The hospital must maintain a medical record for each
>   patient. Records must be retained for at least 5 years.
> - **Content.** The record must document, as applicable: admitting diagnosis;
>   results of all consultations; properly executed informed consent forms;
>   complications and hospital-acquired infections; all practitioner orders,
>   nursing notes, reports of treatment, and medication records; and a discharge
>   summary with the outcome of hospitalization, disposition, and follow-up.
> - **Authentication.** All entries must be legible, complete, dated, timed, and
>   authenticated by the person responsible for the entry.
> - **Timing.** Medical records must be completed within 30 days following
>   discharge.
