Session scoped a two-hour AI fluency workshop for New York Health Information Management Association (NYHIMA) professionals. Core design constraints: mixed roles, no prior knowledge of audience's AI experience, everyone has a laptop, mostly Google accounts for personal use, mostly locked into Microsoft Copilot at work. Workshop goal is fluency — portable habits that survive tool constraints — framed through the Reading-Thinking-Writing (RTW) lens and a learner's permit metaphor. Workbench structure (named directories, artifact capture, model output organization) is the core deliverable participants take home. Workshop materials will stage in people/steve/talks~papers/2026-06-nyhima-ai-for-all/, which currently exists as an empty folder.
