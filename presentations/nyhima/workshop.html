Reading and Writing with AI: Using Large Language Models in Professional Practice

Interrogate the data. Interpret the text. Iterate to refine.

Workshop · June 7, 2026 1:00–4:00 PM · Verona, NY 3 CEUs · NYHIMA
AIX Center  /  Workshop
Overview

A three-hour hands-on workshop for health information management professionals. Participants worked directly with large language models on real HIM material: hospital data from CMS, dense regulatory documents, and their own professional writing. No installation required. No prior AI experience assumed.

The workshop is built around three cognitive operations — Interrogate, Interpret, Iterate — each applied to a different artifact type. Inside every round, participants read, think, and write. The round name marks the dominant move.

Three Operations
Round 1
Interrogate
Working with data
CMS hospital dataset filtered to New York. Ask questions. Surface patterns. The questions are yours — the model helps you see the numbers.
Round 2
Interpret
Working with text
A dense HIM regulatory document. One model reads it and produces an artifact. A second reads the artifact. You decide what each one missed.
Round 3
Iterate
Working with your own draft
Take your best work from Rounds 1 and 2. Hand it to a fresh model. Push back, refine, again — until it sounds like you wrote it.

Before we do anything else — think about the last time AI showed up in your work. Not something you went looking for. A suggested code. A flagged record. A pre-populated field. Who prompted that?
