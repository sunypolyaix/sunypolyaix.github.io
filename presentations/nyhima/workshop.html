Reading and Writing with AI: Using Large Language Models in Professional Practice

Interrogate the data. Interpret the text. Iterate to refine.

Workshop · June 7, 2026 1:00–4:00 PM · Verona, NY 3 CEUs · NYHIMA
AIX Center  /  Workshop
Overview

Today is the how. Using large language models — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude — to read, think, and write in professional practice. The why, the costs, the harder questions: those belong to a different conversation. This one is hands-on.

Three moves: Interrogate data from CMS hospital records. Interpret what you found against a real regulatory document. Iterate the result — 25 words, 100 words, 400 words — until it reads like something you'd put your name on. No installation. No prior AI experience assumed.
