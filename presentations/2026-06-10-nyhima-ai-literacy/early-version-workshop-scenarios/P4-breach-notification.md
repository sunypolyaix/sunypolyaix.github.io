## situation

A staff member accidentally emailed a spreadsheet containing 340 patients' names, dates of birth, and diagnoses to the wrong external address. The recipient confirmed receipt and says they deleted it. Your privacy officer needs to decide whether this is a reportable breach and what the timeline is. You've been asked to summarize the applicable rules.

## source

HIPAA Breach Notification Rule (45 CFR §§ 164.400–414) — Key Provisions

Definition: A breach is an impermissible use or disclosure of PHI that compromises the security or privacy of the information.

Presumption: Any impermissible use or disclosure is presumed to be a breach unless the covered entity demonstrates a low probability that PHI has been compromised based on a four-factor risk assessment:
  1. Nature and extent of PHI involved (identifiers, likelihood of re-identification)
  2. Who used or to whom the information was disclosed
  3. Whether PHI was actually acquired or viewed
  4. Extent to which risk has been mitigated

Notification timelines:
  - Affected individuals: Without unreasonable delay, no later than 60 days after discovery
  - HHS: Same 60-day timeline for breaches affecting 500+ individuals; annual log for smaller breaches
  - Media: Required for breaches affecting 500+ residents of a state or jurisdiction

Discovery date: The date the covered entity knew or should have known of the breach.

Safe harbor: If PHI is secured (encrypted) at time of breach, notification is not required.
