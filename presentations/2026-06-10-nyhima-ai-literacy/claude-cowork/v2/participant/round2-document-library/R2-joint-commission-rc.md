# Round 2 — The Record of Care Standard

**The situation:** A surveyor is coming. Your manager wants a simple checklist
staff can use to confirm that medical records contain everything the Joint
Commission's Record of Care standard requires. You need to turn the standard
into something a chart auditor can actually work from.

**Which model:** Use Claude or ChatGPT (free). Check the result with a second
model.

*The excerpt below is a realistic but fictional paraphrase written for this
workshop. Confirm the current official language against your accreditation
manual before operational use.*

---

## The document — paste this into your first model

> **Record of Care, Treatment, and Services — Standard RC.02.01.01**
> *(paraphrased for training)*
>
> The medical record contains information that reflects the patient's care,
> treatment, and services. At minimum, the record includes:
>
> - The patient's name, address, date of birth, and the name of any legally
>   authorized representative
> - The reason for admission or treatment
> - The patient's initial diagnosis, diagnostic impression, or condition
> - Any findings of assessments and reassessments
> - Any allergies to food or medications
> - Any conclusions or impressions drawn from the medical history and physical
>   examination
> - Diagnoses or conditions established during the course of care
> - Consultation reports
> - Orders for diagnostic and therapeutic tests and procedures, and their
>   results
> - Records of any communication with the patient regarding care, treatment,
>   and services
> - Progress notes
> - The plan for care and any revisions to it
> - Discharge information, including the discharge diagnosis and instructions

---

## 1. Read

> Summarize what this standard requires a medical record to contain. Group the
> items so they are easy to remember.

## 2. Think

> Help me think about which of these items are most often missing or incomplete
> in real charts, and which ones a surveyor is most likely to check.

## 3. Write

> Draft a one-page chart-audit checklist staff can use to confirm a record meets
> this standard. Make each item a yes/no line.
