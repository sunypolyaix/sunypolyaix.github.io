# Round 2 — The Minimum Necessary Standard

**The situation:** A nurse manager is frustrated. Her staff keep getting
requests from insurance companies for "complete medical records" when only
specific information is needed. She wants something she can hand to her team.

**Which model:** Use Claude or ChatGPT (free) for this round — they are strong
with dense text. You will use a second model to check the first.

---

## The document — paste this into your first model

> **45 CFR § 164.514(d) — HIPAA Minimum Necessary Standard (excerpt)**
>
> "A covered entity must make reasonable efforts to limit protected health
> information to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose of the
> use, disclosure, or request."
>
> Guidance notes (from the HHS summary):
> - The minimum necessary standard does **not** apply to disclosures to a
>   health care provider for treatment purposes.
> - It **does** apply to disclosures to health plans, for payment, and for
>   health care operations.
> - Covered entities must develop criteria limiting PHI access to only those
>   who need it.
> - When receiving a request, a covered entity may reasonably rely on the
>   requestor's representation that what is requested is the minimum necessary.
> - Covered entities are not required to make a legal determination about
>   whether a requestor's stated purpose is accurate.
>
> Common tension: insurers routinely request entire records. Covered entities
> are not required to provide more than the minimum necessary, but must not
> impede legitimate treatment or payment.
