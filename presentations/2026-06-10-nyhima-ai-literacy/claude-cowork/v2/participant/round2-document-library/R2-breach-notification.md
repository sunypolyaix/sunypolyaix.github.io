# Round 2 — The Breach Notification Clock

**The situation:** A staff member accidentally emailed a spreadsheet with 340
patients' names, dates of birth, and diagnoses to the wrong external address.
The recipient confirmed receipt and says they deleted it. Your privacy officer
needs to decide whether this is a reportable breach and on what timeline.

**Which model:** Use Claude or ChatGPT (free). Check the result with a second
model.

---

## The document — paste this into your first model

> **HIPAA Breach Notification Rule (45 CFR §§ 164.400–414) — key provisions**
>
> Definition: a breach is an impermissible use or disclosure of PHI that
> compromises its security or privacy.
>
> Presumption: any impermissible use or disclosure is presumed to be a breach
> unless the covered entity shows a low probability that PHI was compromised,
> based on a four-factor risk assessment:
> 1. Nature and extent of the PHI involved (identifiers, re-identification risk)
> 2. Who used it or to whom it was disclosed
> 3. Whether the PHI was actually acquired or viewed
> 4. The extent to which the risk has been mitigated
>
> Notification timelines:
> - Affected individuals: without unreasonable delay, no later than 60 days
>   after discovery
> - HHS: same 60-day timeline for breaches of 500+ individuals; annual log for
>   smaller breaches
> - Media: required for breaches affecting 500+ residents of a state
>
> Discovery date: when the covered entity knew or should have known.
>
> Safe harbor: if the PHI was encrypted at the time, notification is not
> required.
