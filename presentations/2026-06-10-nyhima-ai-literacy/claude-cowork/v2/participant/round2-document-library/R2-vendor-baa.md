# Round 2 — The Vendor Business Associate Agreement

**The situation:** A new transcription vendor sent over their standard Business
Associate Agreement (BAA) for signature. Before it goes to legal, your director
wants a plain-language read on what it actually commits the vendor to — and
where it is thin.

**Which model:** Use Claude or ChatGPT (free). Check the result with a second
model.

*The excerpt below is a realistic but fictional example written for this
workshop. It is not a real contract.*

---

## The document — paste this into your first model

> **Business Associate Agreement — Excerpt (fictional, for training)**
>
> **Section 3. Permitted Uses.** Business Associate may use Protected Health
> Information ("PHI") received from Covered Entity for the purpose of providing
> transcription services, and for Business Associate's own management, data
> aggregation, and product improvement purposes.
>
> **Section 4. Safeguards.** Business Associate will implement reasonable
> administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect PHI.
>
> **Section 5. Subcontractors.** Business Associate may disclose PHI to
> subcontractors and agents, provided such parties agree to substantially
> similar restrictions.
>
> **Section 6. Breach Reporting.** Business Associate will report any breach of
> unsecured PHI to Covered Entity within a reasonable period after discovery.
>
> **Section 7. Return or Destruction.** Upon termination, Business Associate
> will return or destroy PHI where feasible. Where return or destruction is not
> feasible, the protections of this Agreement survive.

---

## 1. Read

> Summarize this Business Associate Agreement in plain language. What is the
> vendor allowed to do with our patient data, and what are they promising?

## 2. Think

> Help me spot the weak or vague spots in this agreement. Which clauses give the
> vendor more room than I would want, and which deadlines or terms are left
> undefined?

## 3. Write

> Draft a short memo for my director: a plain-language summary of what this BAA
> commits the vendor to, plus a bulleted list of clauses I should ask legal to
> tighten before we sign.
