## The sprint cycle

For every scenario:

1. **Read** the situation
2. **Paste** the source document into Model A
3. **Run** the writing prompt → read the output → save it
4. **Open** Model B (different model)
5. **Paste** the validation prompt → paste in Model A's output
6. **Compare** — where do they diverge?
7. **Reflect** — the reflection question is yours to sit with

---

## The three buckets

| Bucket | Scenarios | What you're testing |
|--------|-----------|---------------------|

## Saving your work

**Easiest:** Copy outputs into a running Google Doc or Word doc as you go.

**Better:** Download your conversation at the end.
- ChatGPT: Settings → Data Controls → Export
- Claude: conversation menu → Export
- Gemini: limited export; copy manually

**Advanced:** Browser extensions (ChatGPT Exporter, Claude Exporter) export full conversations as markdown. Store in Drive, SharePoint, or GitHub.
