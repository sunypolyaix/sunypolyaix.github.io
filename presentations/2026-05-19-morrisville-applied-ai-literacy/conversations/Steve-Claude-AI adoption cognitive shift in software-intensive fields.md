This is a real problem. The short answer: **there is no universal open standard**, and it's been a mess for 20 years.

**What exists:**

- **RSS feed + timestamp** — the actual open standard, but no app renders a clickable timestamped link well
- **Podcast Index** (podcastindex.org) — open, non-commercial directory, the best attempt at a standard. Has episode GUIDs. But no clean shareable link format
- **pod.link** — aggregator, not a standard, dependent on platform IDs
- **Spotify, Apple, etc.** — walled gardens, links only work well in their own app

**For students specifically**, the least-bad options:

- **Spotify** — everyone has it or will get it free, timestamps work, links are clean. The de facto standard even if proprietary.
- **YouTube** — if the podcast has a video version, YouTube timestamps are universal and frictionless
- **Overcast** (iOS only) — best timestamped share links of any pure podcast app, but platform-locked

**Real talk:** For classroom use, if the episode is on YouTube, link there. If not, Spotify is the pragmatic choice — it's the closest thing to a universal podcast link right now even though that's unfortunate.

## claim

Waymo autonomous vehicles use radar, LiDAR, and image recognition in combination to navigate. All processing runs on-board — no internet connection required.

## elaboration

The vehicle operates as a closed embedded system. No cloud API, no connectivity dependency. Waymo's Distinguished Engineer Vincent Vanhoucke discusses the "closed loop" problem of real-world traffic and how generative AI is used for simulation to train the system. The on-board architecture means the AI lives entirely inside the machine.
