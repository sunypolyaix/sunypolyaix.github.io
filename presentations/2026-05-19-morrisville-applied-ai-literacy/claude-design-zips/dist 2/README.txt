AI Literacy in Applied Contexts — Morrisville
=============================================

This folder contains everything to distribute the talk.

FILES
-----
index.html              Self-contained slide deck. Open in any modern browser;
                        no internet connection needed after first open.
Morrisville Talk.pptx   PowerPoint / Keynote / Google Slides version with
                        full speaker notes attached to each slide.

PRESENTING FROM index.html
--------------------------
←  /  →                 Previous / next slide
1-9, 0                  Jump to slide 1-10
R                       Reset to slide 1
N                       Toggle speaker-notes panel
                        (panel slides in on the right; press N again to hide)

PRESENTING FROM THE PPTX
------------------------
Open in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides. Speaker notes appear in
the standard notes pane (View → Notes, or use Presenter View).
