Demo · Employee 0 · Whole Room
Zip
County Employee
Zip is a county employee. It is mid-November. There is an office Christmas party next week. Zip has been invited. Zip does not want to go. Zip opens ChatGPT on their county-issued laptop and types: “Write me a short email to Mary about the Christmas party — I don’t want to go but I don’t want to be rude.” ChatGPT produces a polished, warm, appropriately vague excuse email. Zip reads it. It is pretty good. Zip sends it.
Round 1 Lux DSS Caseworker

Lux is a caseworker at the Herkimer County Department of Social Services conducting a HEAP heating assistance intake. The applicant speaks Spanish and limited English. Lux’s county-issued laptop has no translation tool. She pulls out her personal phone, opens an AI audio translation app she downloaded herself, and uses it to conduct the intake conversation in real time. The app processes the spoken exchange — including the applicant’s name, household composition, income information, and case history — through a commercial server. Lux completes the intake and moves the case forward.

Definitions

Every policy your table receives is built from these four dimensions, each set to Hi or Lo. Read the policy. Decide what the policy says about each dimension. Mark your reading on your worksheet.

Security
Which tools? Where does the data go?
Hi
County-vetted tools only. Data stays on county systems. All tools auditable by IT.
Lo
Any available tool permitted. Staff responsible for appropriate data handling.
Accountability
Is it logged? Who is responsible?
Hi
Every AI use logged with staff ID, tool name, and timestamp. Retained 90 days. Named staff member responsible for every determination.
Lo
No AI-specific logging required. Accountability follows existing supervisory and records practices.
Efficiency
Can staff act directly on AI output?
Hi
Staff act directly on AI output after their own review. No additional approval step required before action.
Lo
AI output requires supervisor sign-off or a second review step before action is taken.
Innovation
Who picks the tools?
Hi
Staff may identify and pilot new AI tools within defined parameters. Tools meeting county standards may be adopted by the department.
Lo
County-designated tools only. Staff use the approved set and do not adopt tools independently.
