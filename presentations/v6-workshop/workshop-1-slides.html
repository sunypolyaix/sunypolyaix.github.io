Workshop

June 10, 2026

AI is arriving like an alien species.

We did not decide to invite it. It is here. Like any new species entering an ecosystem, it will change how work gets done, displace certain tasks, and require us to adapt.

They
Decided to create it.
Decided to deploy it.
We
Decide what it means for how we do our jobs.
Set the terms of cohabitation.
Out of scope today

What we are not deciding.

✗Whether AI belongs here. It is already here.
✗Whether it will take jobs. That is a they-level decision.
✗Whether it is good or bad. That question is out of scope.
✓ What the terms of cohabitation should be.
Already decided by county leadership

Three commitments are fixed.

Equity
AI vendors must acknowledge differential impact. Equity is a formal procurement consideration.
Human Impact
AI follows existing supervisory chains. No new approval steps added or removed by this policy.
Infrastructure
Energy use, vendor dependency, and sustainability are formal procurement considerations.

These are not on the table today.

What you are calibrating today

Four operational dimensions.

Hi Lo
Security County-vetted tools only. Data stays on county systems. Auditable by IT. Any available tool. Staff responsible for data handling.
Accountability Every AI use logged: staff ID, tool, timestamp. 90-day retention. No logging required. Existing supervisory practices apply.
Efficiency Staff act directly on AI output after own review. Supervisor sign-off required before action.
Innovation Staff may identify and pilot new tools within defined parameters. County-designated tools only. No independent adoption.

The exercise

Now you do it.

1You receive a policy page labeled with your fruit name. No flavor label -- that is the game.
2Read the scenario on screen. Read your policy. As a table, rank each dimension Hi or Lo.
3Decide: did this employee violate the policy? Circle Yes, No, or It depends. Write your reasoning.
4At reconvene: the flavor is revealed. Compare your ranking to the correct grid.

Round 3
Jam
Public Health Nurse

Jam opens the county's AI prioritization tool on Tuesday morning and follows its ranked schedule for the entire day -- six home visits and two inspections, in the order the tool assigned. She does not log that she used the tool. She does not review the ranking with her supervisor before starting her day.

Did Jam violate the policy?
Round 3 reveal · Jam · Public Health Nurse

The logging question.

Green
VIOLATION — No log + no supervisor schedule review. Both Accountability Hi and Efficiency Lo were breached.
Red
VIOLATION — No log. Accountability Hi requires every use logged with staff ID, tool, and timestamp.
Blue
NO VIOLATION — Accountability Lo means no logging required. Jam acted directly on the AI ranking. Compliant.
Yellow
NO VIOLATION — Any tool, no logging, act directly. Jam's entire day was AI-shaped and the policy permits it.

Jam's day was AI-shaped before she made a single decision. The logging question determines whether there is any record of that.

Did Wow violate the policy?
