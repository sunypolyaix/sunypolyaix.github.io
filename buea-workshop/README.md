# AI Tools for Research
## University of Buea Capacity-Building Workshop

A four-session hands-on workshop for graduate students and faculty in science and engineering exploring practical AI tools for research workflows.

---

## Overview

This workshop equips researchers with practical skills in using AI tools for literature review, data analysis, writing, and coding—all with free, low-bandwidth-friendly tools. Sessions combine conceptual foundations with live demonstrations from real student research projects.

**Audience:** Graduate students and faculty at the University of Buea in science and engineering disciplines.

**Format:** Interactive online sessions with real-world use cases.

**Cost:** Free.

## What We'll Cover

### Literature Review & Source Analysis
Learn to use AI for screening papers, identifying patterns across sources, and extracting key findings faster without losing nuance.

### Data Analysis with AI
From exploratory data work to generating insights, we'll show how AI tools can accelerate analysis while maintaining research integrity.

### Research Methodology
Best practices for using AI responsibly in research design, with attention to limitations and bias.

### Academic Writing Quality
AI-assisted editing, structure refinement, and clarity without compromising your voice or originality.

### AI-Assisted Coding for Research
Write and debug research code (Python, R, MATLAB) with AI pair programming—no experience required.

### Reference Management & Version Control
Organize citations with Zotero; track research artifacts with GitHub. Both free and invaluable.

### Live Demos with Real Student Projects
Every session includes demonstrations drawn from actual student research in your disciplines.

### Free Tools, Low-Bandwidth Friendly
All tools recommended work offline or with minimal bandwidth—critical for varying connectivity conditions.
